# Hack Your Community – Social Media Platform

Hack Your Community is a modern social networking platform designed to connect people, encourage collaboration, and strengthen local communities. The application enables users to create profiles, share posts, interact through likes and comments, and build meaningful connections with others who share similar interests.

The platform focuses on community engagement by providing a space where users can discuss ideas, share knowledge, promote local events, and collaborate on projects. With a clean and user-friendly interface, Hack Your Community makes it easy for individuals to communicate, discover opportunities, and stay informed about activities within their network.

### Key Features

* User Authentication and Secure Login
* Profile Creation and Customization
* Create, Edit, and Delete Posts
* Like and Comment on Posts
* Community Feed with Real-Time Updates
* Responsive Design for Mobile and Desktop
* Modern UI for Enhanced User Experience

### Technology Stack

* Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript / React
* Backend: Node.js, Express.js
* Database: MongoDB
* Authentication: JWT & Cookies

Hack Your Community aims to empower users by transforming social networking into a platform for learning, collaboration, and positive community impact.
